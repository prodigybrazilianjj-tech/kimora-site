💡 How This Works

Submit Order Application does two things at once: logs the application to your database (with confirmation email to the gym), then creates and emails a Stripe invoice directly to the gym owner — with a payment link they can use online.

Tax note: The state rate auto-fills when you tab out of the State field. Most wholesale-for-resale purchases are tax-exempt — if the gym has a resale certificate, set the rate to 0%.

Invoice # tip: Use the format KIM-INV-001 and log it here. That number carries into the Stripe invoice footer so your records stay clean.
