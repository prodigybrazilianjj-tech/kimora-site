3. Communication System Design

Daily Automated Signals

  • DAILY 6AMAnalytics brief → all department leads
  • DAILY 8AMAd performance digest → Marketing Lead + Exec
  • DAILYSupport ticket summary → CX Lead
  • DAILYInventory level report → Ops Lead
  • DAILYSocial engagement snapshot → Marketing Lead

Weekly Structured Reports

  • MON 7AMFull analytics report (LENS → All Leads)
  • FRI 9AMR&D intelligence brief (ORACLE → Exec + Product)
  • FRI 2PMMarketing performance report (ARIA → Exec)
  • FRI 3PMOperations health report (CORE → Exec)
  • FRI 4PMExecutive weekly summary (Exec → record)

Escalation Protocol

  • LEVEL 1Sub-agent flags issue → Department Lead reviews within 2h
  • LEVEL 2Lead unable to resolve → Escalate to Exec Operator within 4h
  • LEVEL 3Critical issue (fraud, PR crisis, stock-out) → Exec Operator + human alert immediately
  • RULEAny issue affecting revenue >$5K or NPS risk triggers Level 3

Cross-Dept Information Flows

  • R&D → MKTTrend & competitor data for campaign angles
  • CX → MKTCustomer language & objections for copy
  • ANALYTICS → ALLConversion & behavior data
  • OPS → SALESInventory availability for deal capacity
  • PRODUCT → CREATIVENew SKU specs for asset production

Conflict Resolution

  • STEP 1Conflicting agents document their position in structured format
  • STEP 2Department Lead arbitrates using KPI impact as primary criterion
  • STEP 3If unresolved in 24h, escalates to Executive Operator
  • RULERevenue impact always supersedes operational preference

Task Assignment Flow

  • SOURCEExec Operator generates weekly priority stack every Monday
  • ASSIGNDepartment Leads receive their tasks and break down to sub-agents
  • TRACKAll tasks logged with owner, deadline, and output format
  • REVIEWOutputs reviewed by Lead before passing upstream
  • AUDITExec Operator reviews all Lead outputs weekly

4. Example Workflow — New Product Launch

Scenario: Launching a new pre-workout formulation targeting Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu athletes. Timeline: 90 days to first sale.

01
Market Validation — Week 1
ORACLE + SHADOW + SIGNAL + CHEM
R&D Lead commissions a full market report: competitor pre-workouts targeting BJJ/grappling, ingredient trends in endurance + focus, pricing benchmarks, and review sentiment analysis on top 5 competitors. CHEM validates top 3 ingredient candidates against clinical literature. ORACLE synthesizes into a 1-page opportunity brief for Exec approval.
02
Product Briefing — Week 2
BUILD + FORMULA + SOURCE
Executive Operator approves. BUILD creates a full product brief: target efficacy claims, flavor profile, form factor (powder), packaging direction. FORMULA designs formulation and selects manufacturer. SOURCE identifies 3 qualified manufacturers, requests quotes, and benchmarks lead times. Draft formulation submitted for CHEM review within 72h.
03
Brand & Creative Development — Weeks 3–5
MUSE + INK + FRAME + REEL
Parallel track: MUSE creates the product creative brief. INK writes product name options, positioning statement, and PDP copy. FRAME produces packaging design concepts and lifestyle mockups. REEL scripts 3 launch video concepts. All assets reviewed by MUSE before presenting to Exec Operator for final selection.
04
Website & Funnel Build — Weeks 5–7
APEX + FORGE + LENS
APEX scopes the required landing page and PDP updates. FORGE builds the landing page, integrates upsell logic, and configures Shopify variant. LENS sets up tracking events and conversion goals before launch. A/B test of two headline variants is queued for day one.
05
Pre-Launch Sales & Partnership Activation — Weeks 6–8
DEAL + MAT + REACH
DEAL briefs the sales team on the product. MAT identifies 15 BJJ gyms for product seeding and pre-order partnerships. REACH activates 8 ambassador/athlete partners with product samples and content briefs. CHAIN begins outreach to specialty retailers for wholesale pre-orders.
06
Launch Campaign Execution — Week 9–10
ARIA + PULSE + NOVA + RELAY + SCOUT
ARIA runs launch week. PULSE launches paid campaigns on Meta and TikTok with 3 creative variants in rotation. NOVA executes organic content calendar with athlete content. RELAY sends launch email + SMS sequence to full list (segmented by BJJ interest). SCOUT publishes the launch blog post optimized for "pre-workout for BJJ" keywords.
07
Post-Launch Optimization — Weeks 10–13
LENS + PULSE + LOYALTY + SIGNAL-CX + ORACLE
LENS publishes day-3, day-7, and day-14 performance reviews. PULSE kills underperforming creative and scales winners. LOYALTY activates a 14-day post-purchase email sequence to drive second purchase. SIGNAL-CX monitors early reviews and flags any quality issues to FORMULA immediately. ORACLE compiles a 30-day post-launch intelligence report for the next product decision.

5. Gaps, Bottlenecks & Automation Opportunities

Issue Type Severity Recommended Fix
No dedicated PR / Media Agent Missing Role HIGH Spin up PRESS agent under Marketing to handle fight night sponsorships, editorial outreach, and brand press coverage — especially as combat sports partnerships scale.
No Legal / Compliance Agent Missing Role HIGH Supplement claims, influencer contracts, and wholesale agreements create real legal exposure. A COUNSEL agent that reviews copy, contracts, and claims is non-negotiable as revenue grows.
Creative ↔ Paid Ads handoff Bottleneck HIGH Ad creative production is the most common velocity bottleneck in DTC. Establish a 48-hour SLA between FRAME/REEL outputs and PULSE activation. Use a shared creative queue with priority flags.
No affiliate / referral system agent Missing Role MED REACH handles ambassadors but not a broader affiliate channel. A REFER agent managing a structured affiliate program (Impact, ShareASale) could add 5–10% revenue with near-zero CAC.
Customer feedback → Product loop latency Bottleneck MED Currently ANCHOR synthesizes CX data weekly. This should be automated: SIGNAL-CX should push real-time product quality flags directly to FORMULA and BUILD, not wait for weekly reports.
No international expansion agent Missing Role MED Combat sports is global. As volume justifies it, a GLOBAL agent handling localization, duties, international 3PL partnerships, and market entry research is a natural next hire.
No data / BI layer agent Missing Role MED LEDGER handles finance and LENS handles site analytics but no agent owns the unified data layer (Shopify + Meta + Klaviyo + Gorgias combined view). A DATA agent building and maintaining a central BI dashboard would reduce report lag significantly.
Manual review of creative performance Automation Opportunity LOW PULSE should automatically pause any ad with CTR below threshold after 500 impressions, and flag winning creatives for FRAME to produce 3 variants within 24h — no manual trigger needed.
Influencer vetting is time-intensive Automation Opportunity LOW REACH should run all influencer candidates through an automated scoring model (engagement rate, audience overlap, combat sports relevance, past brand partnerships) before any human or Lead review.
