"The Mats" — Bear & Octopus Podcast Series

Kimora Co. · AI-generated character video content · Nano Banana production guide · Phase 1 scripts

The Concept

Two characters. One mat. A podcast about jiu-jitsu that subtly makes the case that you need both — and that Kimora is built for both. The Bear represents raw strength, pressure, and physicality. The Octopus represents intelligence, adaptability, and technique. Together, they are the Kimora athlete.

BEAR
Strength · Pressure · Top Game
BJJ GameTop pressure, smashing guard, physical dominance. Believes you can outwork technique with conditioning.
PersonalityConfident, direct, occasionally dismissive of nuance. Learns over time. Good-natured bravado.
VoiceDeep, slow, emphatic. Interrupts when excited. Uses sports metaphors.
VisualLarge, muscular bear in athletic wear — Kimora branded rashguard. Gold accent on gear. Sitting on a mat or stool. Powerful but relaxed.
Role in showThe "raw athlete" perspective. Represents the Kimora athlete who just goes and trains hard.
OCTOPUS
Intelligence · Technique · Guard Game
BJJ GameEight-limb guard work, submissions, transitions, reads opponents. Believes technique always beats strength.
PersonalityThoughtful, slightly nerdy, occasionally pedantic. Gets excited about technique details. Dry humor.
VoiceMeasured, precise, lots of "technically speaking" and "the thing is." Uses multiple arms expressively.
VisualIntelligent-looking octopus in a Kimora Co. branded rashguard. Multiple tentacles active, often gesturing. Holds notes or a tablet.
Role in showThe "thinking athlete" perspective. Represents the Kimora athlete who studies the game deeply.

Show Format — "The Mats"

Episode Length

30–60 seconds per clip. TikTok/Reels native. Each episode is one self-contained debate or topic. No intros longer than 3 seconds.

Structure

Hook (topic dropped immediately) → Bear's take → Octopus's take → Brief back-and-forth → Kimora brand moment or CTA (never forced, always natural).

Setting

A BJJ gym at night. Mats on the floor. Low lighting. Two stools or crates facing each other like a podcast setup. Kimora Co. subtle branding on the wall or gear.

Tone

Entertaining, knowledgeable, never preachy. The characters genuinely disagree and both are right sometimes. The audience picks a side. Comments section becomes the mat.

Kimora Integration

Never say "our sponsor." Instead: Bear references needing energy for his style. Octopus references focus and sharpness. The product need is implied, never sold.

Cadence

2–3 episodes per week during pre-launch. Replace static posts Day 2, 4, 7, 12 with Bear + Octopus episodes. Higher shareability, higher retention.

Production Workflow — How to Make Each Episode

1
Generate character images in Nano Banana using the prompts above. Save 2–3 versions of each character. Pick your canonical Bear and canonical Octopus and save them.
2
Generate the podcast set shot — both characters together. This is your establishing shot used at the opening of every episode.
3
Animate each speaking moment using Image to Video. Feed the character's reference image + the animation prompt. Generate 4–6 short clips (6–8 sec each) per character per episode.
4
Add voiceover using ElevenLabs or similar — or record yourself doing both voices. Bear: slow, deep, confident. Octopus: measured, slightly nasal, precise.
5
Edit in CapCut — cut between character clips on dialogue beats, add captions for every spoken line, drop in subtle ambient gym audio underneath, add KIMORA CO. lower thirds and end card.
6
Export at 1080×1920 (vertical) for TikTok and IG Reels. Post natively on each platform — never cross-post from TikTok to IG (the watermark kills reach).
