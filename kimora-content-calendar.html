Instagram

5x/week. Feed posts Mon/Wed/Fri. Stories daily — polls, Q&As, countdowns. Reels over statics. Best posting windows: 8–10AM and 7–9PM. Engage every comment within 30 min of posting — this is your algorithm lever at this follower count.

Every post with a CTA goes to the coming soon page. Reply to every DM. Batch content in weekly sessions — shoot 5–7 posts at once. Tools to use: Buffer or Later for scheduling. Notion or your calendar to track what's posted. Review performance every Monday.
